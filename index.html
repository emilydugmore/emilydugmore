Hello!

I'm a student at the Centre for Advanced Spatial Analysis (CASA) at University College London. My work sits at the intersection of urban data science, geospatial analysis, and data visualisation. I'm particularly interested in spatial inequalities and using open data to better understand cities and environments. Here are some of the projects I've worked on.

Investigating Disparities in Global Air Quality Monitoring

HTML Website CASA0029 – Urban Data Visualisation

This HTML-based website explores the spatial distribution of air quality sensors across the globe. The site uses interactive maps and charts to tell a research-driven story about the inequalities present in sensor coverage, and investigates potential drivers of these disparities. It highlights a stark divide in urban analytics and data accessibility between the Global North and Global South.

Remote Sensing Learning Portfolio

HTML Portfolio CASA0023 – Remote Sensing Cities & Environments

A nine-week learning diary documenting practical application of remote sensing techniques using Google Earth Engine. Topics covered include spectral indices, land cover classification, SAR analysis, and machine learning approaches to satellite imagery.

Using Pocket Parks to Address Climate and Health SDG Goals in Arsenal

PDF Report BPLN0114 – SDGs, Climate Action & Spatial Planning

A spatial planning report exploring how pocket parks in the Arsenal area of London can contribute to climate resilience and public health Sustainable Development Goals. The analysis draws on open spatial data to assess green space coverage and identify priority locations for intervention.

PlotShare – Making London's Allotments a Shared Space and Resource

Data Story CASA0028 – Designing Spatial Data Stories

An interactive data story examining the distribution and accessibility of allotments across London. The project explores how shared growing spaces could be reimagined as a community resource, addressing food insecurity and green space inequality across the city.

Potential Energy Savings across England and Wales

Interactive Map CASA0007 – Quantitative Methods CASA0029 – Urban Data Visualisation

Using Energy Performance Certificate data, this project maps and models potential energy savings across England and Wales, identifying areas with the greatest opportunity for retrofit investment and highlighting the relationship between housing stock age and energy efficiency.

Municipal Service Delivery Performance in South Africa

React App

A React-based interactive map visualising South African Census data on municipal service delivery. The app highlights spatial inequalities in access to water, sanitation, and electricity across municipalities, drawing on open government data to tell a story about infrastructure inequality.
